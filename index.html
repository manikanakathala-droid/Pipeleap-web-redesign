[Why integrations alone are not enough]

You may already have integrations between your CRM, prospecting and engagement platforms, and some teams have built impressive automation on top. Keep it. Questions

[Before you book]

We already pay for several sales tools. Why add Pipeleap?

Because the return on those tools depends on how much your reps use them. Pipeleap increases that use and removes the admin around it. If it does not create more value than it costs, it has not done its job.

We have integrations and automation in place. What does Pipeleap add?

Integrations move data between applications. Pipeleap handles the work that follows: research, drafting, preparation, follow-up and CRM updates. It works alongside what you have built.

Does Pipeleap replace our CRM?

No. Your CRM stays the source of truth. Pipeleap reads from it and writes activity back to it.

Do reps lose control of what gets sent?

No. Pipeleap prepares drafts and suggestions. Reps review and send.

How do you measure time saved?

Each completed piece of work is credited a fixed number of minutes, and reports mark every figure as measured or estimated. You can replace the default minutes with your own.

Which tools does it work with?

Pipeleap works on top of the tools your team already uses: CRM, email, calendar, prospecting and social selling. We’ll show you how it fits your stack in the walkthrough.
